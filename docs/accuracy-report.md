# BrainWeb lessons vs. the textbook — accuracy report

Source: Freberg, *Discovering Biological Psychology*, 2nd ed. (2010). Method: every chapter's table of contents was
compared with the lessons, and every number/name/claim in the English lessons (bodies, key points, term definitions)
was searched in the extracted book text. Hebrew versions were corrected in parallel.

## 3. Confirmed against the book (sample)
Resting potential −70 mV · threshold −55 mV · myelinated conduction up to ~120 m/s · up to 250,000 new neurons per
minute prenatally · 90% right-handed, ~95% of them with left-hemisphere language · first REM after ~90 min of NREM ·
37 °C set point · Olds & Milner 1954 · MDD and PTSD about twice as common in women · twin/adoption methods,
hypofrontality, dopamine and glutamate hypotheses, MAOA × maltreatment.

## 4. Notes
- Lessons summarise in original wording; no passages or figures are copied.
- A few well-established facts not stated in the book were kept (e.g. H.M.'s surgery in 1953, Gage's accident in 1848).
- The book uses DSM-IV terms (2010); a few lessons mention newer terminology alongside, clearly labelled.
